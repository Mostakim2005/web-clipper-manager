What it does. Takes the page you are looking at — an article, a Facebook post, a Twitter/X post, a Reddit thread, and later a YouTube video or Telegram channel message — and saves it into Obsidian as one note in one consistent shape. The same capture can also leave as a PDF, a full-page PNG/JPEG, or a single self-contained HTML file.

Who it is for. One person, one vault, one dedicated folder.

How it must feel. Click, glance at the preview, save. No account, no sign-in, no telemetry, no cloud round-trip. When the local service is off it degrades politely instead of failing, and a note is never silently half-written or silently overwritten.

What it deliberately is not. The job ends when the note is written. Search, tagging, highlights, reading status, filing and AI all happen downstream in the owner's own workflow.
